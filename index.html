How this page reads AHLT

Man AHL's trend program, from the fund's own daily holdings files

Source. American Beacon publishes AHLT's full holdings every day, and keeps every file since the fund's launch on Aug 30, 2023. This page reads them all.

Futures are counted at notional value, signed: negative is short. Currencies are held through forward contracts; each currency's exposure is the dollar value of its forwards, signed by whether the fund is buying or selling that currency. A negative currency line means the fund is short that currency against the dollar.

Notional is not risk. A 5-year note and a 30-year bond of the same notional carry very different risk, and Man AHL sizes by risk, so the bond line runs large. The fund's own published risk breakdown (by value at risk) is the better guide to what drives its returns; this page shows positions.

Performance does not come from the holdings: they give total NAV, which moves with creations and redemptions, not a per-share return. The Performance panel is the fund's own published NAV, price and month-end returns, read from its page, so it is dated separately from the book.
